Does the verdict hold up?

Backtest: 10,840 declines from 2025-10-01 to 2026-08-01, data the model never saw while training. Graded here: every coin this page has flagged and graded. Coins tagged replay were flagged by replaying CoinMarketCap history from Jul 1, 2026 to Oct 23, 2026, day by day, with a copy of the model trained only on data up to Sep 30, 2025, so it had never seen those months. Everything after that is live.

Read it honestly: the model is good at telling which coins tend to win back their losses (candidates recover about 2.7× as often as "unlikely"), but about a third of every group still drops another 20%+. Candidates are often the more volatile coins: bigger swings both ways. The backtest only includes coins still in today's top 200, so coins that died are missing and real recovery rates are lower. The replay has a smaller version of the same gap (it starts from today's top 200); coins flagged live are graded whatever happens to them.

How it works
1 · FlagAny top-200 coin (no stablecoins) down 25%+ over 30 days on CoinMarketCap data.
2 · ScoreA model trained on 3 years of declines weighs 20 signals: depth of the drop, distance from its 90-day low and high, size, volatility, volume, RSI, Bitcoin's trend and market mood.
3 · RankToday's declining coins are ranked against each other: top 30% Comeback candidate, middle 40% Watching, bottom 30% Unlikely to recover.
4 · GradeRecovered when it gets back to its pre-drop price; otherwise after 60 days: Bounced (+25% at some point), Fell further (20%+ lower) or Still down.

An experiment, not financial advice. Crypto is volatile and coins can go to zero.
