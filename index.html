      How it works

      18 signals. One probability.

      The 7-day call is the main signal. Each day one model (a logistic regression trained on three years of CoinMarketCap data, frozen, then tested on a year it never saw) turns 18 signals about every coin, plus the market's mood, into one number: the chance the coin finishes in the week's top 10 gainers. The 10 highest are the call. The 3-day call uses a simpler 5-signal formula and is marked experimental: in testing it showed no reliable edge after realistic trading costs.

      Momentum

      What's already moving

      Price change over 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days, and how far a coin sits from its 30-day high.

      Independence

      Moving on its own

      Beating Bitcoin, and whether the coin moves with its usual crowd or breaks away from it.

      Volume

      Real money behind it

      Trading volume against its own normal level and against the coin's size.

      Risk

      Size and swings

      Smaller, more volatile coins move further, both ways. The model knows this and the proof page checks it isn't the whole story.

      Overheating

      Has it run too hot?

      The relative strength index (RSI): coins that rose too fast, too recently, often cool off.

      Market mood

      The weather

      Fear & Greed, Bitcoin's share of the market and its weekly trend change how much each signal counts.

      House rules

      Built different, on purpose.

      01

      No confetti

      No streaks, points or celebrations for trading. Regulator studies show that stuff makes people take more risk.

      02

      Every pick graded

      Wins and misses, every single day, with the method written down. No deleting bad weeks.

      03

      Prices locked

      Every call records each coin's price at pick time, so anyone can check the result themselves.

      04

      Honest baselines

      Every number comes with what random picks or the whole market would have done.

      05

      You stay in control

      No auto-trading, no "buy now" buttons. It's information. What you do with it is your call.

      06

      Motion you can stop

      Anything that moves on this page can be paused, and it respects your reduced-motion setting.
