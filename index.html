        How it works

        Five signals. One score.

        These five signals are the building blocks, shown with the original weights. The 3-day model now uses backtest-tuned weights (BTC-relative 79%). The 7-day model v2 adds 13 more signals, such as peer momentum, volume trend and size, and adjusts for market mood using Fear & Greed and BTC dominance. Every pick and every result is public.

        House rules

        Built different, on purpose.

        01

        No confetti

        No streaks, points or celebrations for trading. Regulator studies show that stuff makes people take more risk.

        02

        Every pick graded

        Wins and misses, every single day, with the method written down. No deleting bad weeks.

        03

        Prices locked

        Every call records each coin's price at pick time, so anyone can check the result themselves.

        04

        Honest baselines

        Every number comes with what random picks or the whole market would have done.

        05

        You stay in control

        No auto-trading, no "buy now" buttons. It's information. What you do with it is your call.

        06

        Motion you can stop

        Anything that moves on this page can be paused, and it respects your reduced-motion setting.
